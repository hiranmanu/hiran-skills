# Question Bank Methodology

## Part A — Likely questions they'll ask (Phase 3)

Generate from two inputs, not one:
1. **The JD** — every must-have and every explicitly named competency
   becomes a likely competency-based question.
2. **The research (Phase 1)** — anything unusual in the company's recent
   news/strategy (a pivot, a tough quarter, a new market) tends to surface
   as "how would you handle..." or "what do you know about us" framing.

Standard/Deep sets should span these categories, not just competency
questions:
- Motivation ("why us / why this role / why now")
- Core competency (2-4, mapped directly to JD must-haves)
- Behavioral/STAR (2-4, pulled from `ip-background.md`'s story bank)
- Curveball/weakness ("tell me about a failure", "what's a gap for you") —
  answer honestly using `ip-background.md`'s "Notable Gaps" section rather
  than a fake humble-brag weakness.
- Company-specific ("what would you do differently about X we just
  announced") — only include if Phase 1 research surfaced something
  concrete enough to answer credibly.

For each question, produce a STAR answer:
> **Q:** [question]
> **STAR:** *Situation* — [1 line]. *Task* — [1 line]. *Action* — [1-2
> lines]. *Result* — [1 line, with a real metric from `ip-background.md`
> if one exists, otherwise say "no hard metric on file — describe the
> outcome qualitatively" rather than inventing a number].

Rapid mode: 2-3 lines total per question (Situation+Action blended, one
Result line), not the full 4-beat structure — there isn't time to read a
full STAR answer back in a 10-minute prep window either.

## Part B — Smart questions to ask them (Phase 4)

**The test this has to pass:** could this question have been asked by
someone who hadn't done any research? If yes, rewrite it. Every question
in Standard/Deep mode must reference a specific fact from Phase 1 research
— a headline, an earnings-call line, a stated strategic priority — not a
generic "what's the culture like" or "what does success look like."

Pattern:
> "I saw [specific fact from Phase 1, with rough source — 'in your Q2
> call' / 'in the funding announcement' / 'on your site's roadmap page'].
> How does this role fit into / support / get affected by that?"

Genuine beats clever — the goal is demonstrating real homework plus
authentic curiosity, not a gotcha question. Avoid anything that could read
as testing the interviewer.

Tier by seniority if the interview stage is known (see `ip-config.md`):
- HR/recruiter: process, team structure, what a strong first 90 days
  looks like — lighter on strategy, since an HR screener often can't
  answer deep strategic questions anyway.
- Hiring manager: team-level priorities, how this role's success is
  measured, near-term roadmap.
- Panel/exec: strategic direction, competitive positioning, the specific
  earnings-call or funding-announcement detail from Phase 1 Deep research.

Rapid mode: 3 questions, still each tied to a Phase 1 fact — just fewer of
them, not lower-quality ones.

## Part C — HR Screen Basics

**Mandatory whenever the interview stage is HR/recruiter screen**, in
every speed mode (see `ip-config.md`'s stage table) — an HR screen fails
on missed basics far more often than on strategic depth.

Prep tight, direct answers (not scripts) for:
- "Tell me about yourself" — 60-90 second version, pulled from Phase 5's
  intro.
- "Why this role / why this company" — must reference Phase 1 research,
  not generic enthusiasm.
- "Why are you leaving / left your current role" — honest, forward-framed,
  no criticism of a past employer.
- "Salary expectations" — ask the user for their number/range before
  answering on their behalf; don't invent a figure.
- "Notice period / availability" — ask the user; don't assume.
- "Right to work / visa status" — ask the user if not already known; don't
  assume a status.
- "Any questions for me?" — 1-2 of the lighter-tier smart questions from
  Part B, not the strategic ones (an HR screener usually can't answer
  those, which reads as a mismatch of question to audience).

If the user hasn't given salary/notice/visa specifics and there's no time
to ask (Rapid mode, interview imminent), flag it as "you'll need to have a
number ready for this" rather than guessing one.
